# School Visit Report

## Dengue Awareness and Environmental Education Activities

## 1. Introduction

On May 20 and May 21, school visits were carried out as part of an educational awareness activity focused on dengue prevention, environmental care, forest fire prevention, and local wildlife protection.

The activities were designed for students and aimed to present important health and environmental topics in a simple, practical, and engaging way. The visits included dengue awareness talks, an origami activity, the participation of forest rangers, demonstrations of firefighting tools, and a presentation of local wildlife specimens.

![General view of the school visit and awareness activity carried out on May 20 and 21.](images/page-01-image-01.png)

**Figure 1.** General view of the school visit and awareness activity carried out on May 20 and 21.

## 2. Objectives of the Activity

The main objective of the school visits was to raise awareness among students about dengue prevention and environmental protection.

The specific objectives were:

- To explain the importance of preventing dengue and avoiding mosquito breeding sites.
- To encourage students to share prevention practices with their families and communities.
- To promote learning through a creative dengue origami activity.
- To raise awareness about forest fire prevention.
- To introduce the role of forest rangers and the tools they use in their work.
- To educate students about local wildlife and safe behavior when encountering wild animals.

![Students participating in the dengue prevention awareness talk.](images/page-02-image-01.png)

**Figure 2.** Students participating in the dengue prevention awareness talk.

## 3. Dengue Awareness Activity

During the visits, students received information about dengue prevention and the importance of eliminating places where mosquitoes can reproduce. The activity emphasized that stagnant water in containers, bottles, tires, plant pots, or other objects can become breeding sites for mosquitoes.

Students were encouraged to keep their homes, schools, and communities clean, remove unused containers, cover water storage recipients, and inform their families about prevention measures.

![Educational materials used to explain dengue prevention measures to students.](images/page-03-image-01.png)

**Figure 3.** Educational materials used to explain dengue prevention measures to students.

## 4. Dengue Origami Activity

As part of the dengue awareness activity, students participated in a creative origami activity related to dengue. Each student received paper sheets and informational flyers so they could make their own origami and take the material home.

This activity allowed the students to learn about dengue prevention in a more visual, creative, and participatory way. The use of origami helped reinforce the prevention message while keeping the students engaged.

## 5. Participation of the Forest Rangers

Forest rangers also participated in the school visits. They explained their role in environmental protection and presented the main elements of their uniforms and work equipment.

During the activity, the students learned about the tools used by forest rangers in the prevention and control of forest fires. The explanation helped students understand the importance of this work and the responsibility involved in protecting natural areas.

![Forest rangers explaining the function of their uniforms and equipment to the students.](images/page-05-image-01.png)

**Figure 6.** Forest rangers explaining the function of their uniforms and equipment to the students.

## 6. Forest Fire Prevention

The forest rangers gave an educational talk about how to prevent forest fires. They explained that fires can be caused by careless actions such as leaving embers burning, throwing trash in natural areas, or using fire irresponsibly.

The students were encouraged to avoid actions that may cause fires and to inform adults if they notice dangerous situations. The activity highlighted the impact of forest fires on animals, plants, people, and the environment.

![Demonstration of tools and equipment used for forest fire prevention and control.](images/page-05-image-02.png)

**Figure 7.** Demonstration of tools and equipment used for forest fire prevention and control.

## 7. Local Wildlife Awareness

As part of the environmental education activity, the forest rangers brought preserved animal specimens from the local area. These specimens were used as educational material to show students some of the species that can be found in the region.

The students were taught to respect wild animals, avoid touching or approaching them, and seek help from an adult if they encounter a potentially dangerous animal. The activity helped promote awareness of local biodiversity and safe behavior in natural environments.

## 9. Results of the Activity

The school visits allowed students to learn about dengue prevention and environmental protection through interactive and educational activities. The origami activity helped reinforce the dengue prevention message in a creative way, while the participation of the forest rangers expanded the activity to include forest fire prevention and local wildlife awareness.

The students were able to observe real materials, ask questions, participate in the origami activity, and interact with the topics presented. This contributed to a better understanding of dengue prevention, environmental responsibility, and safe behavior around wild animals.

## 10. Conclusion

The school visits carried out on May 20 and May 21 were valuable educational activities for raising awareness among students. Through clear explanations, creative activities, and practical demonstrations, the visits addressed important topics such as dengue prevention, environmental care, forest fire prevention, and respect for local wildlife.

The combination of dengue awareness, origami, forest ranger participation, and wildlife education created a meaningful and engaging experience for the students. These activities helped strengthen the connection between schools, the community, and prevention initiatives related to public health and environmental protection.
