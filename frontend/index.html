PUBLIC HEALTH CONTEXT

Dengue in Brazil — A Growing Crisis

Brazil is the country most affected by dengue in the world. In 2024, over 10 million cases were recorded — a historic record — along with nearly 6,000 deaths.

10M+ cases in Brazil in 2024
~6,000 deaths recorded in 2024
50% of all Latin American cases
4 serotypes circulating simultaneously

Source: World Health Organization (WHO) and Pan American Health Organization (PAHO).

THE PROJECT

What is Pakarina?

Pakarina is an interdisciplinary initiative developed at the Federal University of Ouro Preto (UFOP) in partnership with UCASAL and Yncrea.

Our Mission
