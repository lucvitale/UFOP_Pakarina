About Us

THE PROJECT

The Project

Pakarina is a spatio-temporal platform created in 2026 through the collaboration of professors and students from multiple academic institutions.

MISSION

Mission

Pakarina aims to georeference arboviral occurrences and bring together information, tools, devices and people currently operating in separate environments, recognizing that the dynamics of these diseases are determined by interconnected factors.

ENVIRONMENT

Environmental Context

Climate variability influences abundance and vector competence, while unplanned urbanization and insufficient sanitation systems increase contact between humans and vectors.

ONE HEALTH

One Health Approach

The project adopts an integrated perspective that considers human health, environmental conditions and the factors that influence the transmission of arboviral diseases.

DISEASES

Arboviruses and Public Health

Arboviral diseases represent a complex public health challenge involving environmental, epidemiological and social factors.

ARBOVIRAL SURVEILLANCE

Emerging Threats

More recently, Oropouche virus has received increasing attention due to its rapid geographic expansion in the Americas. Its transmission is primarily associated with biting midges, creating complex epidemiological and ecological challenges for arboviral surveillance.

PLATFORM

Technological Platform

At the core of the project, Pakarina develops a modern web platform capable of integrating information from sensors, surveillance devices, databases, mapping systems, external services and educational resources, creating an evolving and adaptable technological base.

FUTURE

Vision

Our vision is to build a sustainable technological ecosystem that strengthens surveillance capabilities, improves information accessibility and promotes collaboration between technical and non-technical participants, connecting people, information and action.
