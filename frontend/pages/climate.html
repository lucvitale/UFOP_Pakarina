Environmental conditions favoring mosquito proliferation

Temperature Dengue mosquitoes thrive between 25°C and 35°C.
💧
Humidity Humidity above 60% favors mosquito breeding.
💨
Wind Speed Low wind speeds allow mosquitoes to remain active.
🌧️
Precipitation Rain increases mosquito breeding sites by creating standing water.
