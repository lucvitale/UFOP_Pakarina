Dengue Awareness and Environmental Education

We carry out awareness activities in several schools with the aim of providing clear, practical, and engaging information about dengue prevention and environmental care. Through educational talks, creative activities, and interactive experiences, we encourage habits that contribute to the health and well-being of the entire community.

How Can We Prevent Dengue?

During the activities, students learn that the mosquito responsible for transmitting dengue can reproduce in containers with stagnant water, such as bottles, tires, flower pots, and buckets. We promote simple actions that help eliminate breeding sites and protect the community.

✅ Eliminate standing water
✅ Cover water storage containers
✅ Check tires, bottles, and flower pots
✅ Keep yards and surrounding areas clean
✅ Share prevention information with family

Learning Through Creativity

As part of the awareness activities, students participate in a creative mosquito origami workshop. This activity provides a visual, practical, and interactive way of learning, turning an artistic experience into an educational tool that reinforces dengue prevention messages.
